About the school

Education that shapes the whole person.

The Heritage International School is building an inclusive and inspiring learning community where every child is seen, heard and valued.

Our approach brings together academic understanding, character, confidence and creativity. Students are encouraged to ask questions, explore ideas, express themselves and take responsibility for their learning.
